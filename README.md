# Lab 2 Results

### Topic

For this assignment, I chose to look at impacts of recent weather on California and Washington. To perform my searches using the YouTube crawler, 
I made searches using "California drought", "atmospheric river", and "Washington storms" as my search prompts. I wanted to make this comparison
because of some similarities that I noticed between the two states. Namely, the fact that the recent atmospheric rivers played a part in the weather
of both states. 

### Word Clouds

Overall, there is a general pattern in the words between my three word clouds. Of course, since my topic is about the atmosphere and storms, 
a substantial portion of the words in all three clouds are related to some form of rain or water. However, there are still some words unique
to each cloud based on their individual themes. The atmospheric river word cloud is more general, while the two about either state have words relating to
local conditions: drought and dryness-related words for California and floods and storms for Washington. 

### Improvements

In the future, I think that I can improve my research using this crawler method by changing what site I gather information from. For this assignment,
I used YouTube, which isn't exactly the most reliable or academic source available. There are also bound to be some videos that aren't entirely related to 
the topic I want to look at. 

### Interesting Things

From this exercise, the most interesting thing to me was what I learned about the state of California's drought. According to the sources I found, the last
time California had 0% abnormally dry or drought conditions was back in 2000. This meant that these conditions existed for pretty much my entire early life 
and a few of my early teenager years. It was definitely a surprise when I saw that the same holiday season downpour here had also drenched Caalifornia.
